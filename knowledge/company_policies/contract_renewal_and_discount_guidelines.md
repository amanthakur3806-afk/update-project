# Commercial Policy: Contract Renewals, Grace Periods & Volume Discounts

## 1. Enterprise Renewal Horizons & Milestones
- **90-Day Renewal Horizon**: Account executive initiates formal commercial health check and feature utilization review.
- **60-Day Renewal Horizon**: Delivery of contract renewal proposal and multi-year pricing models.
- **30-Day Renewal Horizon**: Final legal and procurement approval window.
- **Grace Period Policy**: Customers receive a 14-calendar-day service grace period after contract expiration before traffic rate-limiting or suspension.

## 2. Multi-Year & Volume Discount Schedules
- **1-Year Commitment**: Standard list pricing.
- **2-Year Multi-Year Agreement**: Up to 10% discount on baseline platform subscription.
- **3-Year Multi-Year Agreement**: Up to 18% discount on baseline platform subscription + waived TAM onboarding fees.
- **High-Volume Telemetry Tiers**: Accounts processing over 50M API events/month qualify for tiered burst discounts of 25%.

## 3. Churn Mitigation Authority & Escalations
- For accounts identified with elevated churn risk or active contract renewal horizons (e.g. Stripe November 2026 renewal, Shopify December 2026 renewal), Account Executives (Sarah Jenkins, Alex Chen) are pre-authorized to offer:
  - 1 quarter of complimentary Platinum TAM coverage.
  - Dedicated latency tuning and infrastructure optimization by platform engineering (e.g. eBPF socket bypass, Vitess shard balancing).
  - Flexible semi-annual or quarterly wire billing terms.
  - Pre-approved Black Friday or holiday burst allowances with zero overage penalty multipliers.
